Profile & Photos

Free to use for posters, programmes and press. Please credit the photographer where one is named.

Minchan Kim is a jazz drummer and composer based in Seoul, New York and Europe. Grounded in a powerful sense of swing and a clear hard bop language, he performs actively on the international stage. Born in Daejeon, he began playing drums at eight and made his professional debut at nineteen. Since then he has played around 200 shows a year, performing in about 100 cities across 35 countries in the United States, Europe, East Asia, North and Central America and Africa.

After majoring in drums in the Department of Postmodern Music at Kyung Hee University (2005), he built his professional experience in musical theatre bands, orchestras, pop recording sessions and broadcast stages. In 2007 he met the German bassist Martin Zenker and set out on the path of jazz in earnest, joining New York saxophonist Jesse Davis's Korea tour as his drummer. The concert recorded at EBS Space on that tour was released on the German label Nagel Heyer as Soul Searchin': Live in Seoul, and for several years afterwards he toured Europe with Davis and Zenker.

He broadened his musical foundation with a master's degree in jazz drums from the University of Music and Performing Arts Munich (Hochschule für Musik und Theater München, 2023). He was named a Rising Star by the monthly magazine JazzPeople in 2012 and was an international scholar of the CJ Cultural Foundation. In 2023 he won second prize at the Kurt Maas Jazz Award, establishing himself as a player to watch on the international jazz scene.

After completing his master's degree he moved to New York, where he has established himself as a professional on the local jazz scene. At leading New York clubs including Smalls Jazz Club, Mezzrow, Jazz Forum and Ornithology, he has shared the stage with Jesse Davis, Anthony Wonsey, Antonio Hart, Steve Carrington, Brandi Disterheft and Will Lyle, among others. In 2025 he appeared at the UNESCO International Jazz Day Festival in Angola with Steve Carrington Trio Infinity and toured the US West Coast with the Anthony Wonsey Trio. Based in New York, he also leads his own band, the Minchan Kim Trio.

He has performed with world-class musicians including Don Menza, Jesse Davis, Kenny Washington, Peter Washington, Tony Lakatos, Jim Snidero, Alain Jean-Marie, the Belmondo Brothers, Terumasa Hino and Damon Brown, and is a much-loved sideman whom these artists call on again and again. He has also appeared on a number of recordings, including the Damon Brown Quintet's Han River Tales.

As an educator, he has taught jazz drums at Hanyang University, Baekje Institute of the Arts and Seoul Jazz Academy, and served as music director of a Korean–German jazz exchange program (2022–2023). He currently teaches in the jazz performance departments of Hanyang University and Dankook University. Faithful to the essence of traditional jazz drumming while naturally blending in a contemporary sensibility, his playing is building an ever stronger presence on the international jazz scene.
